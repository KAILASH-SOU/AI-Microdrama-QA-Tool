## 3 Tips for a Flawless Video Take:
1. **Pacing:** Speak in a calm, clear, confident tone. Don't rush—180 seconds is plenty of time for ~440 spoken words.
2. **Smooth Cursor Guidance:** Use your mouse cursor like a laser pointer. Hover steadily over the metric or card you are discussing rather than moving it erratically.
3. **Emphasize the Value Proposition:** Put extra vocal emphasis on **"pre-render"**, **"paywall danger zone"**, **"zero-leakage blind voice test"**, and **"dialogue doctor"**.
